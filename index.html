Comprehensive Expert Questionnaire
Validation and Assessment of barriers, Indicators, and Enablers Affecting sustainability in perishable food supply chains (PFSCs).

Objective

The objective of this study is to identify, validate, and assess the barriers, indicators, and enablers influencing sustainability, resilience, and their integration in perishable food supply chains (PFSCs). The expert responses will help to:

  1. Assess the impact and importance of various barriers and indicators.
  2. Validate the correct categorization of sustainability and resilience dimensions.
  3. Develop a comprehensive framework integrating sustainability and resilience for PFSCs.

Disclosure

This questionnaire is designed solely for academic and research purposes. All responses will remain strictly confidential and will be used only in an aggregated form for analysis. No individual or organization will be identified. Participation is voluntary, and experts may withdraw at any stage. The researchers declare no conflict of interest.
