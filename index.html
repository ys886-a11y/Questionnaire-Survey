Objective

This study identifies, validates and assesses the barriers, indicators and enablers influencing sustainability, resilience and their integration in perishable food supply chains (PFSCs). Your responses will help to:

  1. Assess the impact and importance of the barriers and indicators.
  2. Validate the categorisation of sustainability and resilience dimensions.
  3. Develop a framework integrating sustainability and resilience for PFSCs.

Disclosure

This questionnaire is for academic research only. All responses are strictly confidential and will be analysed only in aggregate; no individual or organisation will be identified. Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw at any stage. The researchers declare no conflict of interest.
